Robust Intelligence Awarded IDIQ Contract to Eliminate AI Risk for the US Air Force
Case Studies
Author
Authors
Kojin Oshiba
Kojin is a co-founder of Robust Intelligence.
Eliminating AI Risk for our customers is the mission of Robust Intelligence. Our indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity (IDIQ) contract for up to $950,000,000 with the U.S. Department of Defense will allow critical applications of Artificial Intelligence within the Joint All Domain Command and Control to leverage Robust Intelligenceâs technology to eliminate AI Risk.
Robust Intelligence has been awarded a multiple-award indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to compete for orders under a $950,000,000 ceiling for the maturation, demonstration and proliferation of capability across platforms and domains, leveraging open systems design, modern software and algorithm development in order to enable Joint All Domain Command and Control (JADC2). This contract is part of a multiple award multi-level security effort to provide development and operation of systems as a unified force across all domains (air, land, sea, space, cyber, and electromagnetic spectrum) in an open architecture family of systems that enables capabilities via multiple integrated platforms.
Robust Intelligence is backed by Tiger Global, Sequoia Capital, Engineering Capital, and Harpoon and has raised $44M in funding. Our customers include PayPal, Expedia, Medtronic, NTT Data, and Tokio Marine.
